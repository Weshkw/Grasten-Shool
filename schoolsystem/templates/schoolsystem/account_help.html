Account help

Accounts are issued by the school office, so that only the real student or member of staff can see their results and fee records.

  • New student or staff member? Ask the office for your account and a temporary password, then change it after you log in.
  • Forgotten your password? Visit or call the office; they will confirm who you are and set a new temporary password.

Call or message the office on +254 711 716 221.
